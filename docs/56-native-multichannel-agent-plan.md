## Product and execution contract

The normal mandate and agent chat remain the user interface. CLI tests use those same production tools, SDK, durable commands, exact review and dispatch paths, with a strict local provider replacing Unipile. A separate test planner or SQL approval bypass cannot satisfy acceptance. Real connected accounts are the intended final product; simulator accounts are a local verification boundary.

Each person can have verified email, WhatsApp and LinkedIn identities. Default outreach is staggered. Any verified reply cancels pending outreach across all their channels, invalidates stale approvals/grants and records the person-level transition. A request already submitted to a provider cannot be unsent: retain its original immutable binding and receipt, reconcile it, and hold competing effects while its outcome is unknown. A desired conversational reply requires a fresh proposal. Own-sent notifications, ambiguous aliases and unverified identities are not inbound replies.

Keep the existing sequence builder and n8n runtime for users choosing structured sequences. One recorded owner advances a person at a time; agent and n8n must not independently send. Human review is the default. Booking readiness creates the chat notice/task for a human to send the booking link.

## Current primary API findings

Checked the official Unipile documentation on 17 October 2026 and compared the existing shared social runtime and relationship adapters.

| Area | Contract and implication |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp identity | Provider internal identity may be an opaque `@lid`; public phone identity uses `@s.whatsapp.net`. Resolve the canonical phone and corroborate aliases within the connected account; never derive a phone from an arbitrary LID. The existing PN-only initial lookup needs explicit LID acceptance work, with ambiguity remaining blocked. [Users overview](https://developer.unipile.com/docs/users-overview). |
| LinkedIn identity | Public profile slugs differ from internal IDs. Classic, Recruiter and Sales Navigator use different product identifiers; obtain the appropriate provider ID with the account/product-scoped profile request. Do not silently switch product or subscription. [Retrieving users](https://developer.unipile.com/docs/retrieving-users), [profile API](https://developer.unipile.com/reference/userscontroller_getprofilebyidentifier). |
| First message | `POST /chats` sends the first message as part of chat creation. Treat it as an effect requiring exact approval, account/peer authority and durable journalling. Existing-chat sends must validate the account, direct chat, attendees and recipient. [Sending messages](https://developer.unipile.com/docs/send-messages), [new chat API](https://developer.unipile.com/reference/chatscontroller_startnewchat). |
| Invitations | LinkedIn invitation is a separate effect with pending/accepted state. A chat after an invitation note does not prove acceptance: a recipient may reply without accepting. The relation webhook can arrive hours later; use the current account-scoped relationship proof. InMail/product credits require explicit support and authority. [Inviting users](https://developer.unipile.com/docs/invite-users), [detecting acceptance](https://developer.unipile.com/docs/detecting-accepted-invitations). |
| Inbound events | `message_received` also contains the user's own sent messages. Compare authenticated account and sender identity, then corroborate full message/chat/attendees. Initial connection does not emit all historical messages; reconnect may emit offline arrivals. Deduplication and complete history are separate from receiving a webhook. [New messages](https://developer.unipile.com/docs/new-messages-webhook). |
| Reconnect/readiness | GET data can be stale when account status is not OK. Creation/reconnection and synchronization are different events; Classic and premium products may sync separately. Match the exact connection generation and required product; old coverage cannot prove readiness. [Account lifecycle](https://developer.unipile.com/docs/account-lifecycle). |
| Limits and uncertainty | Vendor recommendations vary by account/product and do not establish a general sending entitlement. Retain configured quotas, budgets and pacing. No provider guarantee was found that makes an uncertain social mutation safely retryable. The architectural rule is to keep the original journal/receipt and avoid blind retry. [Provider limits](https://developer.unipile.com/docs/provider-limits-and-restrictions). |
