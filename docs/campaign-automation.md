# Campaign automation

## WhatsApp campaign delivery

WhatsApp steps now compile to the same authenticated `/api/internal/campaigns/step` callback as email and LinkedIn. Acquire renders each message and sends through the sequence owner's existing WhatsApp service connection. Publishing requires `WHATSAPP_SERVICE_URL` and `WHATSAPP_SERVICE_SECRET`; enrolment requires a connected WhatsApp account and a contact phone number with an explicit international country code. Personalized messages are limited to 4000 characters.

WhatsApp uses the existing campaign send claims, contact holds, sending windows and shared mailbox caps/spacing. Provider submission IDs are recorded before advancing; callback replays do not resend recorded messages. Unresolved attempts and ambiguous provider responses are held for review rather than automatically retried. n8n owns waits and retries for scheduling holds. Gmail remains required for campaign enrolment and email reply monitoring. WhatsApp submission confirms provider acceptance, not delivery or reading. The delivery adapter itself requires no migration. Cross-channel reply monitoring is described below and remains off until its migration and rollout are approved.

## Cross-channel reply monitor (migration 0016)

`drizzle/0016_campaign_reply_monitor.sql` was applied to the local OrbStack database (`127.0.0.1:5434/project_acquire`) on 6 October 2026 with explicit approval. It was also applied to production Supabase project `njasjqpztiteionfxxul` on 6 October 2026 with explicit approval. Production preflight verified all 14 existing ledger entries against repository hashes; only 0016 was executed and recorded, bringing the ledger to 15 entries. The pre-existing WhatsApp tables were verified without rerunning migrations 0014 or 0015. All five new tables, eight app RLS policies, campaign role grants and the committed 0016 hash were verified. This schema change does not deploy or activate the reply monitor. It adds four app tables for contact identity snapshots, deduplicated inbound receipts, canonical contact holds, and account checkpoints/leases, plus the WhatsApp inbound metadata journal. The app tables use forced RLS with owner-scoped writes, organisation-scoped contact holds, and the existing restricted campaign worker role. Composite contact/organisation foreign keys prevent cross-organisation identity links. No existing campaign/version/enrolment data is rewritten.

The app defaults to `CAMPAIGN_REPLY_MONITOR_MODE=off`, which preserves existing Gmail reply checks. `observe` records additional channel replies and proposed decisions without creating additional holds; Gmail's existing stop behaviour still runs. `enforce` atomically records a reply and holds the contact, stopping remaining active, paused and review enrolments across channels. Confirmed replies retain the existing email hold too. Shared channel identities with separate person/email groups hold the candidates for identity review rather than asserting a reply belongs to one of them. Contact identity snapshots remain after phone/email/profile edits, and canonical holds prevent reenrolment with a changed email. These holds are not automatically resumable.

Contacts using WhatsApp/LinkedIn actions require those monitors. Connected optional channels are also discovered for contacts with usable identities, so a WhatsApp reply can stop an email-only sequence. Explicitly disconnected, never-monitored optional accounts are skipped; service failures and previously monitored disconnections cannot certify an empty inbox. LinkedIn contact URLs are resolved to verified member URNs. WhatsApp phone numbers require an international country code; sender LIDs must have a verified phone mapping. Once an account identity is pinned, changing the underlying provider account requires review rather than silently advancing the old checkpoint.

The existing minute scheduler advances account scans and registers existing enrolments, including paused/review cases. Every pre-send callback checks the applicable channels again. Events and page checkpoints commit in the same transaction; only a complete scan advances freshness. Per-account renewable leases fence stale workers. Changing the registered identity set invalidates the scan lease and freshness. Sends recheck canonical holds and monitor health immediately before submission; monitoring older than five minutes, partial history, missing identities, disconnections and provider errors hold outreach. A send already submitted cannot be recalled.

Gmail scans use paginated contact batches and a durable watermark with a two-minute overlap. Receipts deduplicate overlap and provider replays. WhatsApp uses `CAMPAIGN_REPLY_MONITOR_ENABLED=true` on the channel service **after** the approved migration; live and history events are persisted under the session lease before `/replies` can declare a check complete. Pending notifications must be synchronized. Incoming text, voice and media count; self-sent messages, groups, broadcasts, protocol messages and reactions do not. Unresolved sender aliases remain in the journal and block successful checks rather than being discarded.

LinkedIn uses `LINKEDIN_REPLY_MONITOR_ENABLED=true` on its service. Open Messaging and a conversation in the owner's browser to capture the existing conversation and message read queries. `/replies` replays only those observed queries, respecting the account read budget, and resumes conversation/message pagination from the encrypted owner vault. It persists inbound metadata without bodies. Self-sent messages, group conversations and connection acceptance do not count as replies. Unknown response shapes, missing pagination, throttling and stale browser sessions hold checks. **The reader must still be validated against the intended live account's response shapes before enforcement; fixture tests do not establish live provider compatibility.** Large or slow scans may exceed the five-minute freshness window and will hold sends.

The worker-authenticated `/api/internal/campaigns/replies` endpoint also accepts individual normalized events. It never accepts an arbitrary contact ID, verifies owner membership and a previously pinned account identity, and acknowledges only after the receipt/hold transaction commits. It does not advance account freshness; a complete account scan is still required. `/api/inbound/reply` continues serving the older outreach schema and is not used for campaign ingestion.

The campaign studio shows reply channel/time and each account's last successful check/error. A manual “Retry safety checks” action is available for preflight failures; it does not override reply/ambiguous-send holds or skip the next checks.

## n8n sequence runtime (migration 0013)

Publishing with `CAMPAIGN_N8N_ENABLED=true` creates and publishes one n8n workflow per immutable sequence version. The workflow contains a Webhook trigger, named email steps, a Wait node for each sequence delay, and Wait nodes for temporary send holds. A contact enrolment starts one execution of its pinned version. n8n owns the execution and timing; Acquire receives each step callback, checks Gmail replies and suppression, applies mailbox caps and business hours, then sends through the contact owner's Gmail grant. Workflow payloads contain the enrolment and version IDs, not Gmail tokens or email bodies.

Migration `drizzle/0013_campaign_n8n_runtime.sql` was applied locally and in production with specific approval. It adds the workflow ID to versions and a runtime/dispatch marker to enrolments. Existing enrolments default to `legacy`; the existing minute scheduler continues sending those and scanning replies. New n8n enrolments are dispatched by that scheduler, while their send steps are driven by n8n. A failed dispatch remains unstarted and is retried on the next tick. The sync endpoint provisions workflows for versions published before cutover without moving their current contacts.

Cutover completed on 25 September 2026: migration 0013 was applied with specific approval; the code, `CAMPAIGN_N8N_URL`, `CAMPAIGN_N8N_API_KEY`, `CAMPAIGN_N8N_WORKER_CREDENTIAL_ID`, and `CAMPAIGN_APP_URL` were deployed; authenticated `POST /api/internal/campaigns/sync` created the workflow for the previously published version; and `CAMPAIGN_N8N_ENABLED=true` was deployed. The production Header Auth credential sends the existing `OUTREACH_WORKER_SECRET` to the Acquire domain. The scheduler workflow remains published for legacy contacts, n8n dispatch and reply scans. The production n8n plan cannot restrict API-key scopes, so the user explicitly authorized a non-expiring full-scope key for ongoing sequence publishing. Rotate it in n8n and Vercel together if it is ever replaced.

The production `First Sequence` v1 workflow is active at `https://n8n.mlai.au/workflow/0IQl0zTZ8GFXykNk`. A webhook execution with a nonexistent enrolment reached Acquire through the Header Auth credential and returned the expected 404; this checked authentication without sending Gmail. The campaign studio shows the n8n workflow link. The mandate's production mailbox is disconnected, so a live Gmail send from that account has not been tested.

## Safety rules

- Sends occur only on weekdays from 09:00 to 17:00 Australia/Melbourne, at least 60 seconds apart per mailbox, and under the mailbox's rolling 24-hour cap.
- Before each send, Acquire checks the contact record, organisation-wide email holds, legacy suppressions, the sender's own Gmail grant, and the sender's Gmail inbox for new inbound mail. The first email also checks for any prior Gmail conversation; follow-ups hold if an unrecorded manual outbound email appeared after subscription.
- An inbound message creates a deduplicated event and an organisation-wide hold for that email address, stopping every active enrolment for it. Manual pause is resumable. A reply stop is not automatically resumable.
- The `(enrolment, step)` send record is unique. If a Gmail request's result is ambiguous, it is marked `unknown` and no automatic retry occurs. This avoids double-send; reconciliation and manual review are required before continuing.
- A contact becomes exclusively assigned to the subscribing user for campaign outreach. The database rejects an enrolment whose owner does not match that assignment or the Gmail connection owner.
- LinkedIn and WhatsApp delivery use the same Acquire send guards and contact holds. Their reply collectors remain disabled until the cross-channel reply-monitor rollout above is completed.
