## The five facts that matter to the build

**1. He is the mandate.** Since July 2025 he's been running a self-funded ETA search: acquire
and operate **one** Australian SMB, **$1–5M EBITDA**, strong fundamentals, owner thinking about
succession or exit. That's not a persona — it's a live search, and it is almost certainly the
mandate M0 will be tested with.

This substantially answers A3 and A4. It also sizes the problem: $1–5M EBITDA in Australia is
roughly $10–50M revenue, 20–200 staff. That's not "1M+ AU companies", it's a few thousand
nationally. The universe is small enough to be tractable — and precise enough that a screening
agent returning near-misses will be obviously wrong to him.

**2. "Owner thinking about succession or exit" is the hard part, and Firmable almost certainly
doesn't have it.** Firmable has firmographics, revenue bands, employee counts, tech stack,
buying signals. It does not have "the founder is 62 and has run this for 28 years with no
successor". That signal has to be *derived* — ASIC director records (age, appointment date,
tenure, sole-director structures), LinkedIn tenure, ownership concentration, absence of a
second-generation director. Building that inference, with evidence attached to it, is the
actual intellectual work of the Screening agent. Everything else is filtering.

**3. He has a finance degree in banking and corporate finance.** The Finance agent's model and
indicative terms will be read by someone who can check them. No free pass on hand-waved
figures, and "evidence or nothing" is a standard he can personally enforce.

**4. He ran outbound programs for a living.** Sumo Logic full-cycle rep, led an agency
partnership for outbound SDR, built GTM motions from zero at four companies. The Outreach agent
will be judged by whether a working sales operator would actually send the draft. Competent-but-
generic AI email fails this bar instantly. Personalisation has to come from the evidence the
Screening agent gathered, not from a tone instruction in the prompt.

**5. He worked directly with ANZ at DataCo — a 1835i portfolio company.** 1835i is ANZ's
venture arm, and he "partnered closely with the founding team and ANZ Bank (a key channel
partner and stakeholder)". The unnamed "major Australian business bank" is very likely ANZ, and
the relationship is warm and pre-existing rather than cold. That's good news for requirements
landing mid-build — but it also means the bank relationship is *his personal capital*, which
raises the cost of shipping something that embarrasses him in front of them.
