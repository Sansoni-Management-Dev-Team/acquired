## Most consequential findings

1. **Maintenance scope mismatch is a verified local configuration gap.** The
   enabled unattended research-maintenance service's owner and organization do
   not match any of the four recent user workflows. It therefore cannot provide
   their scoped recovery. The db48 run retained97 queued jobs across multiple
   observations. This is evidence of missing unattended coverage, not proof that
   one setting is the sole cause of every queued job. Preserve owner scoping;
   provision/verify the intended actor rather than weakening guards.
2. **Field coverage is much weaker than company counts imply.** An earlier db48
   detail snapshot contains seven finished public-field result objects:43 field
   outcomes,42 failed and one reported. It also contains successful page reads
   and cooldown recoveries. That narrow sample disproves complete enrichment,
   but must not be extrapolated to every company or treated as proof a site
   publishes every requested fact.
3. **People settlement includes incomplete work.** The sampled roots have
   settled people aggregates with49/29/153/41 cancelled or incomplete admissions.
   Provider-found rows, root saved counts and all available linked contacts are
   different quantities. Emails/phones/employment freshness require their own
   checks; contact counts are not deliverability proof.
4. **Workflow complete is an orchestration state.** Run233 is complete while its
   company root remains partial and53 child/root jobs are partial or failed.
   Completion does not mean all facts or contacts were successfully obtained.
5. **Requested count is not a hard discovery/fan-out cap.** Finite exploration
   policies can produce100–211 companies despite smaller desired targets. Do
   not describe every excess candidate as a duplicate or budget violation; do
   reconcile the product target, actual saved policies and expected cost.
6. **Simulator service, app dispatch, reply ingestion, saved history and browser
   rendering are separate tests.** Direct hosted replies succeeded on all three
   channels. The complete production Acquire chain is still not certified.
7. **Sequencing proof is layered and dated.** Historical native cadence and
   cross-channel reply stops include real simulator receipts, human approvals
   and some scripted race inputs. They do not certify unattended approvals,
   naturally elapsed warm follow-ups, actual n8n wait restart or real Unipile.
8. **New UI behavior is verified locally.** The chat welcome/Start, clean URL,
   pre-Start reload and exact retries were browser-tested with admission
   intercepted. Existing real26 October research results prove the underlying
   research path, not a new full provider run under that final chat fixture.
